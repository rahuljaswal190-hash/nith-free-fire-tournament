Rulebook

Rules for a fair NIT Hamirpur tournament.

These rules are designed for a student-organized campus-style Free Fire event. Admins can update exact timings, maps, payment instructions, and prize distribution before the tournament starts.

1. Eligibility

  • The event is hosted around NIT Hamirpur and is open according to organizer announcement.
  • Every player must use the submitted Free Fire name and ID only.
  • One player cannot play for multiple teams in the same mode unless admins allow it.

2. Roster and formats

  • Battle Royale has separate Solo, Duo, Trio and Squad sections.
  • Solo requires 1 player, Duo requires 2 players, Trio requires 3 players, and Squad requires 4 players.
  • Clash Squad requires a four-player team. Clash Squad room capacity is 2 teams.
  • No roster change after slot confirmation unless approved by admins.
  • Captain/contact person is responsible for payment reference, room updates, and teammate discipline.

3. Clash Squad

  • Normal and One Tap options are available.
  • Each Clash Squad room plays only one match.
  • The winning team is rewarded after result and fair-play verification.
  • One Tap weapon/settings restrictions will be announced before the match.

4. Battle Royale

  • Each Battle Royale lobby plays 3 matches.
  • Solo lobby capacity is 48 players, Duo is 24 teams, Trio is 16 teams, and Squad is 12 teams.
  • Top 3 entries/teams are selected by cumulative score.
  • Placement points and kills decide ranking; penalties may be applied for rule breaks.
  • Players/teams must join on time and play with registered players only.

5. Fair play

  • No hacks, scripts, third-party advantage tools, config abuse, bug exploitation, teaming, or match-fixing.
  • No emulators unless organizers announce a separate emulator category.
  • Abusive voice chat, threats, hate speech, or offensive names can lead to disqualification.
  • If suspicious gameplay is reported or observed, the concerned player must provide a clear, unedited screen recording covering the relevant match period for verification. Failure to provide requested recording/proof may result in score cancellation, disqualification, or further action by the admins.

6. Room ID and check-in

  • Room ID/password are shared only through official private communication.
  • Players should be ready at least 15 minutes before match time.
  • Late teams may lose the slot after the waiting time announced by admins.
  • Internet, battery, updates, and device issues are the player’s responsibility.

7. Fee, prize and refunds

  • Battle Royale entry tiers are ₹20, ₹40, ₹60, ₹80 and ₹100. Clash Squad starts directly from ₹50.
  • Solo fee is per player. Duo, Trio, Squad and Clash Squad fee is per team.
  • Prize is entry-based and may vary if confirmed entries/payments are fewer than expected.
  • ₹20 Battle Royale full-lobby target reward pool starts from ₹200, subject to final organizer confirmation.
  • Refunds are handled by organizer policy and are usually not available after slot confirmation unless the match is cancelled.

8. Admin authority

  • Admins can reject suspicious entries, wrong UIDs, duplicate players, unpaid entries, or abusive teams.
  • Admins’ decision is final for disputes, score corrections, penalties and prize payout.
  • Public leaderboard updates happen after result verification.
  • This is not a Garena-sponsored event.
