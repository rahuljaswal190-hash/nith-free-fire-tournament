Battle Royale

Solo, Duo, Trio, Squad Battle Royale.

Players can join alone or with a team. Each Battle Royale lobby has up to 12 numbered entries/teams (slots 1–12). Every lobby plays 3 matches and the top 3 entries/teams are selected after verification.

Pick your format

Separate sections for every team size

Choose Solo if you do not have a team, Duo for two players, Trio for three players, or Squad for a complete four-player team.

Battle Royale format

Each lobby holds up to 12 entries/teams (slots 1–12). Three matches are played in its assigned time slot. Current IST timing appears beside each lobby and can be updated by the organizer. Top 3 entries/teams are selected by cumulative kills plus placement points.

Room ID/password will be shared privately. Screenshots/recordings may be required for result verification.

Battle Royale Registration

Entry starts from ₹20. Solo fee is per player; Duo, Trio and Squad fee is per team. A lobby slot number is reserved when you register; your approval status remains pending until the organizer reviews it.
