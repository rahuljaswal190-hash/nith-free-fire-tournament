Clash Squad

Normal or One Tap Clash Squad.

Each Clash Squad room is one match between two sides. Choose 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, or 4v4, then register only the players required for that size. Normal and One Tap modes are available.

Before you register

Enter a valid Free Fire UID and IGN for each player required by your selected side size. The fee tier is charged once per registered side/team, regardless of whether it is a 1v1 or 4v4. Room ID/password will be shared privately after verification.

One Tap settings/weapons will be announced by admins before the match. If a player uses banned settings or unfair tools, the team can be disqualified.
