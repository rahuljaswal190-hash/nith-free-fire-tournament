Leaderboard

Track teams and verified scores.

Battle Royale ranks Solo, Duo, Trio and Squad entries by placement points plus kills across three matches. Clash Squad ranks by match win points and round difference. Results appear after admin verification.

Battle Royale scoring

3 matches per lobby. Total = placement points + kills - penalties. Top 3 entries/teams are selected for Solo, Duo, Trio and Squad sections.

Placement table: 1st 12, 2nd 9, 3rd 8, 4th 7, 5th 6, 6th 5, 7th 4, 8th 3, 9th 2, 10th 1, 11th-12th 0.

Clash Squad scoring

1 match per room. Win = 3 points, loss = 0. Tiebreakers: round difference, then admin decision/proof.

Normal and One Tap leaderboards use the same ranking logic unless admins announce a special rule.
